# Code of Conduct

Sheriff welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Discuss the work without attacking the person.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and make room for different perspectives.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and other people's time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to Dagim Shimelis at [dagimshimelis1992@gmail.com](mailto:dagimshimelis1992@gmail.com). Include relevant links, what happened, and any immediate concerns. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports and limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate and respond. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent restriction on participation. Enforcement applies to all participants.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
